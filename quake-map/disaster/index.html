Earthquake damage estimate

Drag a box over any place (up to about 30 km across) to download its buildings and use them. The server needs internet access.

The cyclone track is a straight line through the chosen point in the direction of travel.

Pick a depth raster for the flood you want to test, for example a return-period map. Nothing is bundled.

Advanced: fault direction

Leave blank if unknown. With a strike, the quake is a rectangular rupture along that direction and shaking stretches along the fault.

Rake: 0 strike-slip, 90 reverse (thrust), -90 normal.

Or drag the diamond marker, or shift-click the map. The epicentre can be outside the city.

3D shows each building at about 3 m per storey. The lowering option is a visual cue for expected collapse, not a prediction of how any building falls.

The report has the scenario, summary with ranges, priorities, access, dispatch order, methods and caveats.

Drag a box to choose what to save. A click without dragging saves the whole view.
